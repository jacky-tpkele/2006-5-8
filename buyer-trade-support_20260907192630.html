04 / Incoterms

Compare & Select a Trade Term

Choose one preferred term. If you are unsure, select “Not Sure” and provide your destination — this is usually better than guessing.

Not Sure

Let TPKELE review your shipment context and suggest a practical term.

EXW

Buyer controls pickup and most onward logistics arrangements.

Usually suitable for
  • Experienced importers with their own forwarder
  • Buyers wanting strong logistics control

FCA

Seller hands goods to the buyer’s carrier at the agreed named place.

FOB

Common sea-freight term with seller-side export and loading responsibility.

CFR

Seller arranges ocean freight to the named destination port.

CIF

Seller arranges ocean freight and the insurance required by the agreed term.

DAP

Delivery to the named destination; buyer handles import clearance and duties.

DDP

More complete destination delivery; availability depends on route and import feasibility.

What may add preparation time?

  • New paper-box artwork or private-label design approval
  • Special plastic packaging or new label structure
  • Large quantities or consolidated product lists
  • Additional document or inspection requirements

How can buyers speed up confirmation?

  • Provide exact product/model and expected quantity
  • Select packaging and documents on this page
  • Provide destination port/city and target delivery date
  • Send artwork or reference packaging when available

09 / FAQ

Buyer FAQ

Common order-planning questions are collapsed by default to keep the page easy to scan.

Can I request custom packaging before I have final artwork?

Yes. Select the packaging type first and describe your intended style. Final artwork and feasibility can be confirmed before mass production.

Can I request packing dimensions before placing the order?

Yes. This is useful for freight estimates, warehouse planning and container analysis. Final values should match the confirmed product and packaging combination.

Should I select EXW, FCA or FOB if I already have a forwarder?

You can compare the trade-term cards above. If you are still unsure, select “Not Sure” and provide your destination and logistics arrangement for review.

Can I select PI, CI, Packing List and Certificate of Origin together?

Yes. Select all documents you expect to need. TPKELE can then clarify which are relevant at each order and shipment stage.

Can I request L/C?

You can select L/C as a preference. Availability and detailed banking conditions depend on the specific order and will need commercial review.
